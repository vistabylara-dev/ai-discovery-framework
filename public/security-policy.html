Security disclosure policy

Vista by Lara welcomes responsible reports of suspected security vulnerabilities affecting its public services or the AI Discovery Framework repository.

How to report

Email solution@vistabylara.com with a concise description, affected URL or component, impact, and steps to reproduce. Do not include other people's personal data or secrets in a report.

Research guidelines

  • Test only systems and accounts you own or are authorized to assess.
  • Do not access, alter, or retain data that is not yours; avoid denial-of-service, social engineering, and physical testing.
  • Stop testing and report promptly if you encounter personal data or a security control failure.
  • Allow reasonable time for triage and remediation before public disclosure. Coordinate any publication with the affected maintainers.

This policy does not grant authorization to test third-party services and does not promise a response time, reward, or legal safe harbor. Include contact information so the report can be acknowledged and clarified.
